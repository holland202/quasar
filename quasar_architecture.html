The one open, honest finding

The error-driven curriculum is confirmed wrong by design and left in the repo unchanged, on purpose — it's the experiment that produced the finding. The fix (progress-proportional weighting) is verified to work at a larger private-lab scale and verified to not work at this repo's public demo scale. Both results stand; per the README, the variable is scale, not signal. The documented next step is retesting at larger scale, not swapping the formula again.
